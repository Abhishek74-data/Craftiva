- **Greeting message:** "Namaste 🙏 Welcome to Craftiva Furniture — a 20-year Kirti Nagar workshop making custom beds, wardrobes & furniture, factory-direct across Delhi-NCR. Tell us what you're looking for (and your room size) and we'll send a quote."
- **Away message:** "Thanks for messaging Craftiva! We usually reply within a few hours (workshop hours 10am–7pm). Share the item + your size and we'll quote you."
- **Quick-reply labels (free CRM):** New lead · Quoted · Ordered · In production · Delivered.
- **Ask every lead to *save your number*** so you can add them to a Broadcast list (256/list) for new designs & offers.
- Send a **branded quotation PDF** (Refrens, free) instead of a plain text price — it makes you look like a brand, not a sub-contractor.
