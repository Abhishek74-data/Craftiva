## 2. The thesis: capture the middleman's margin

| Party | Role | Profit/bed | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Craftiva | Makes the bed (~₹35–40k cost) | **₹4–5k** | All the work |
| Showroom/designer | Owns the customer, resells ₹60–70k | **₹25–30k** | A phone call |

Growth ≠ making more furniture. Growth = **selling DIRECT to NCR customers** to capture the ₹20–30k the middleman takes.

### The capacity math (the motivating number)
Factory ceiling ≈ **10 beds/month**. 
- All wholesale: 10 × ₹4–5k = **₹40–50k/month**.
- All direct: 10 × ₹12–15k = **₹1.2–1.5L/month** — from the *same* factory, same effort. Add wardrobes (10–15/mo) and fully-direct ≈ **₹1.5–2.5L/month** vs ~₹50–70k now.

> **You don't need to go viral. You need ~10 direct orders a month. Your factory can't even make more than that right now.** Target = **~2 direct customers/week.**

### Positioning
> **"Craftiva — factory-direct custom furniture. We build what Delhi's showrooms sell. Designer quality, honest factory prices."**
Verified craft (real 20-yr workshop, factory video) = the moat Kingsman fakes.

## 3. Direct sell-prices (research-benchmarked, Delhi-NCR)

**Use a 3-tier quote for every product** so customers self-select and margin is protected:
1. **Value** (ply/engineered) — matches Kirti Nagar street prices, wins price-shoppers.
2. **Premium (solid sheesham) — the money-maker.** Priced 15–25% under Wooden Street/Pepperfry/showrooms.
3. **Custom/designer** — bespoke sizes/finishes, quote per project, highest margin.

| Product | Direct price (premium/solid) | Your margin | Beats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bed** (solid-wood, storage/hydraulic) | **₹48,000–55,000** | ₹8–20k (2–4× today) | Showroom ₹60–70k by ₹10–20k |
| Bed (ply/engineered w/ storage) | ₹28,000–34,000 | — | Wooden Street start ₹20,999 |
| **Wardrobe/almirah** (solid, 3–4 door) | **₹38,000–52,000** | high | Wooden Street ₹59,999, Sona Arts ₹42,599 |
| Wardrobe (ply/laminate 3-door) | ₹22,000–30,000 | — | — |
| Sliding wardrobe (laminate) | ₹28,000–40,000 | — | Modular ₹1,850–3,000/sq ft |
| Dining set (solid 6-seater) | ₹42,000–55,000 | — | Wooden Street ₹79,999, Pepperfry ₹82,999 |
| Study table (solid + storage) | ₹9,000–20,000 | — | *(upsell, not a lead product)* |

**Put prices ON the page/catalogue** — the transparent-price makers are exactly who win these searches. Lead marketing with **beds + wardrobes** (highest ticket, biggest margin capture, and what you make best).

## 4. CRITICAL correction: heavy furniture stays DIRECT-LOCAL, not marketplaces

Research verdict: **Amazon/Flipkart will NOT recover your bed margin — they just swap the showroom for Amazon.**
- Beds/wardrobes = **"Heavy & Bulky"** (>72 in or >22.5 kg). Amazon furniture referral **10%** (₹5,000 on a ₹50k bed) + 1% TCS + closing + freight + high damage/return risk ≈ **₹7–10k drag** — roughly the showroom's cut, and you eat two-way freight if it arrives damaged. No national Easy Ship for bulky items.
- **Same bed direct in NCR:** Porter/own tempo ₹500–1,500, you install on-site, zero referral, zero return-freight. **Keep the full ₹10–15k.**

**Rule:** Bed/wardrobe/big table → **direct NCR delivery + install.** Only **light SKUs** (side/bedside tables, stools, flat-pack chairs, shoe racks, shelves, compact study tables) go on marketplaces — **later, post-GST.**

## 5. GST — when, not if

- **Not needed to start.** Direct NCR selling (WhatsApp/Instagram/UPI/cash) needs **no GST** until ₹40 lakh goods turnover. GBP also needs no GST.
- **Needed for:** (a) selling on Amazon/Flipkart (mandatory from ₹1), (b) GST invoices to designers/offices/corporates, (c) input-tax-credit on plywood/hardware (18% → creditable).
- **Cost/time:** ₹0 govt fee + ₹1–3k CA, **~7 days** via Aadhaar-OTP route. File quarterly (QRMP).
- **Plan:** Start direct-local now GST-free. Register GST in ~30–60 days when ready to list light SKUs + invoice B2B. Catch: once registered you charge 18% on all sales incl. B2C — a reason to keep pure-cash local heavy sales outside GST until deliberately ready.

## 6. Reviews — your #1 free growth lever (you have 1)

- Reviews = **~19% of local Maps ranking**; **star floor 4.5+, winners 4.8–4.9**; **velocity beats count** (3–5/week steady > 40 in one week, which gets flagged fake).
- **Honest constraint:** your current "customers" are B2B showrooms who won't leave consumer reviews. The review engine **starts with your first DIRECT customers.** Seed with genuine past *direct* buyers only — **never fabricate** (FTC penalty up to $53k/violation; Google can wipe all reviews).
- **Never** offer discounts/gifts for reviews, and **never review-gate** (both banned, both nuke your profile).
- **Method:** free Google-review **QR code** (Canva, ~₹200 to print on a delivery card) + WhatsApp the review link the day after every delivery. Reply to 100% of reviews within 48h.
- **Benchmark (20-min manual step):** on Google Maps, search "furniture shop near me" + "wardrobe/bed maker Kirti Nagar" — note the top-3's review counts/ratings = your 6–12 month target. Matching ~50–70% + weekly velocity usually breaks into the pack.
- **Trajectory:** Day 30 ≈ 10–15 reviews at 4.7★+, Day 90 ≈ 20–40 with visible velocity.

**Review-request WhatsApp script:**
> "Namaste [name] 🙏 Thank you for choosing Craftiva for your [bed/wardrobe]. We've just started our Google page — if you were happy with the piece and our service, a one-line Google review (and a photo of it in your home, if possible!) would mean a lot to our workshop. Here's the link 👉 [QR/link]. Thank you so much!"

- **Skip Shopify** (~₹21k/yr — overkill). When inquiries are steady, add **Instamojo (₹0/mo, pay-per-sale)** or **Dukaan Premium (~₹9k/yr)** for a browsable catalogue.
- **Spend the freed budget on 15–20 professionally-lit product photos + one hero showpiece**, not subscriptions.
- **Honest-photo rule:** every listing = 1 plain un-staged photo + **L×W×H dimensions** + a scale-reference shot (pillow/bottle). Use AI only to clean backgrounds, never to generate/warp the product. This kills the #1 furniture complaint ("smaller than pictured").
